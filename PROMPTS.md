## Tips

- Generate at 4:3 or 1:1, at least 2048 px, then downscale for the web.
- If text comes out garbled, regenerate or fix the text in an editor —
  AI tools often mangle small type.
- Keep the navy (#0A1A33) and orange (#F5821F) values in the prompt; they are
  the brand anchors across all three lines.
